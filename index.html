Abstract

RL has become a practical language for modern LLM research: aligning models with preferences, strengthening reasoning, training agents through interaction, and optimizing multimodal systems. This tutorial introduces RL from the perspective of LLMs, using prompts, tokens, rewards, verifiers, preference data, and trajectories as the central objects.

The reading path follows the paper's current logic: first establish the LLM and RL foundations, then build a PPO-style RLHF workflow from a concrete LLM training example, then discuss improved rewards, advantage estimation, efficient methods, DPO, and finally the extensions to reasoning, agents, and multimodal models.

Chapters

  1. Introduction PDF

    Motivates why RL has become important for LLM alignment, reasoning, agents, and multimodal models.

  2. Preliminary: LLMs and RL PDF

    Reviews autoregressive LLMs, pre-training, prompting, SFT, inference, MDPs, and the mapping between LLM generation and RL.

  3. Understanding RL in LLM Training PDF

    Uses a homework-assistant example to derive policy gradient, temporal decomposition, variance reduction, importance sampling, PPO, reward models, and the complete RLHF workflow.

  4. Improved RL for LLMs PDF

    Addresses the main pressure points after basic PPO: reward quality, reward sparsity, reward generalization, advantage estimation, efficiency, and direct preference optimization.

  5. RL for Reasoning PDF

    Connects test-time scaling, process verification, MCTS, iterative RL, large-scale RL, and on-policy distillation for reasoning models.

  6. Agentic RL PDF

    Studies how RL helps LLM agents learn planning, tool use, environment interaction, credit assignment, memory, skills, and trajectory refinement.

  7. Multimodal RL PDF

    Introduces RL for multimodal understanding, visual reward models, diffusion generation, and flow matching generation.

  8. Conclusions and Future Directions PDF

    Summarizes the paper and highlights RL for pre-training, efficient training, scaling limits, and continual self-evolving RL.

  9. Appendix: Systems and Datasets PDF

    Lists RL systems, preference datasets, reasoning data, and agent environments discussed in the paper.
